Radicalizing Design: A Socialist Manifesto

  1. Confront, contest, and reconcile your positionality. Design must stand for/on something. It must not stand on the backs of the people your privilege crushes.
  2. Resist against the logic of commodification. Design must be for use-value (for users) instead of exchange-value (profit).
  3. Indulge in the world of co-production and create a cultural commons full of open-source technology, speculative creations, and hack the system. Design to redesign and un-design.
  4. Design must rage against the machine with bravery. It must cultivate virtues of feminism, anti-racism, and anti-capitalism.
  5. Your work must be humble. You are of this world, not the world itself. Embrace it with gratitude and learn.
  6. Resist the aesthetics of exploitation which communicate and beautify the interests of those in power. Your design must not reproduce and fortify the ideology of capital.
  7. Good design does not fall for the politics of pretty. It is design that is equitable, accessible, and emancipatory.
  8. Individual recognition does not change the social structure. Liberate your work from superficial identity politics and let it liberate us all instead.
  9. Design manifestos must be aggressive and radical. Design manifestos must not rest on edginess, innovation, and high-tech aspirations. Design manifestos must manifest.
